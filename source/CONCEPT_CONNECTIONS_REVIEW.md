# Concept connections review

Updated 2026-09-29 for the 138-concept pilot. Every lesson has an explicit editorial connection record. There are 196 inline prerequisite links and 335 selected forward applications.

“Used in” points only to later lessons. Inline prerequisite mentions point to earlier lessons. Both represent dependency edges from the earlier idea to the later idea; the combined graph is exported as `dist/concept-graph.json` and checked for cycles. The graph contains the 138 implemented lessons; the 256-concept outline is still a proposal for the rest.

The pilot removes 25 backward application links rather than silently reversing their meaning. Those historical pairs are recorded in expansion-batch.json. Applications are curated, not generated by linking every matching term. A lesson has at most three inline prerequisite links and four applications; endpoint lessons may have none. Original lesson text, all 1,280 original questions and their stable IDs are retained.

Transformer still leads to language models, mixture of experts, fine-tuning and KV caching. The LLM overview links back to Transformer and forward to RLHF. The added linear-algebra bridges connect vectors and dot products to projection, rank, SVD, PCA and autoencoders.
